    How to play: each round, decide whether you want to farm guard or plunder. Each farmer produces 100 points for themselves base. However:

    • If there is more plunderers than guards, the plunders steal all the round's points and divide them between themselves
    • Each guard "taxes" each farmer 20 points for defense. If they actually defend an attack (there is more than 0 plunderers) they take 40 (each) from each farmer

    Whoever has the most points at the end wins. Good luck!
